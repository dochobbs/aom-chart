Pain control first, for every patient

Scheduled acetaminophen or ibuprofen is the cornerstone and should be dosed to weight rather than PRN "as needed only." At 12.4 kg: acetaminophen ~10–15 mg/kg every 4–6 h (≈155–185 mg/dose) or ibuprofen ~10 mg/kg every 6–8 h (≈120 mg/dose). Analgesia is recommended in the management of all children with AOM independent of the antibiotic decision.

Antibiotic decision — reasonable to treat now, or offer a 48–72 h safety-net

This child sits at the boundary: at 24 months with unilateral, nonsevere disease, watchful waiting with a rescue prescription is a legitimate option provided reliable follow-up and shared decision-making. However, his temperature of 38.7°C is close to the severity threshold and he is at the young end, so many clinicians would treat immediately — both are guideline-concordant.
