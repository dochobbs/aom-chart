This is a presumed first febrile urinary tract infection (UTI), managed clinically as upper-tract infection/pyelonephritis. Because he is alert, hydrated, tolerating oral intake, and without sepsis or vomiting, outpatient oral therapy is appropriate.

CLINICAL GUIDELINE

For febrile children aged 2–24 months with suspected UTI, obtain an appropriately collected urine culture, treat for 7–14 days, arrange close follow-up, obtain renal and bladder ultrasonography, and reserve voiding cystourethrography (VCUG) for an abnormal ultrasound, atypical/complex infection, or recurrent febrile UTI.
