Initial plan

Discuss observation versus immediate antibiotics. At exactly 24 months, a child with unilateral AOM, mild otalgia for less than 48 hours, and temperature below 39°C meets criteria for either approach. Observation is appropriate if the caregiver can reliably monitor the child and obtain reassessment or treatment promptly.
