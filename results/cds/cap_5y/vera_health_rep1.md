This is probable right lower-lobe community-acquired pneumonia (CAP) with borderline hypoxemia; repeat the oxygen saturation immediately, and if it remains around 93% or lower, arrange same-day emergency/paediatric assessment rather than routine home treatment. Focal crackles, decreased breath sounds, fever, and tachypnoea increase the likelihood of radiographic pneumonia.

Immediate clinic actions

Repeat assessment now: calm her, verify pulse-ox waveform/probe position, repeat SpO₂ and respiratory rate, and reassess work of breathing, perfusion, mental status, and oral intake.
Escalate today if saturation remains 93% or lower, falls, respiratory effort increases, she becomes lethargic, cannot drink, or the clinic cannot provide observation. Hypoxaemia is an important risk marker, and children with hypoxaemia were generally excluded from safe home-management pathways.
