Antibiotic regimen

High-dose amoxicillin 80–90 mg/kg/day divided BID is first-line. He has no penicillin allergy, no amoxicillin in the prior 30 days, and no purulent conjunctivitis, so amoxicillin (not amoxicillin-clavulanate) is preferred and has the lowest failure/recurrence rates among agents.

Symptom control

Scheduled acetaminophen and/or ibuprofen for otalgia and fever; analgesia is recommended for all AOM patients regardless of the antibiotic decision. Ibuprofen is appropriate at 24 months and provides longer-lasting relief; ensure adequate hydration.

Follow-up and return precautions

Reassess in 48–72 hours if not improving. Counsel the mother (well-positioned as a pediatric nurse) to return promptly for persistent or rising fever, new lethargy or decreased activity, ear drainage, or postauricular swelling/tenderness — signs concerning for mastoiditis or treatment failure.
