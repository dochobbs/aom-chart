Assessing amoxicillin dosing in young children with AOM

The AAP 2013 guideline recommends high-dose amoxicillin at 80 to 90 mg/kg/day in divided doses for acute otitis media, with consideration of penicillin allergy and prior antibiotic exposure. The dosing regimen must be tailored to age, weight, and risk of resistant pathogens.
