Completed

This presentation is consistent with acute otitis media (AOM) of the right ear meeting diagnostic criteria: moderate TM bulging, effusion with impaired mobility, and acute onset of ear pain in the setting of a viral URI.

For a 24-month-old with unilateral AOM without severe symptoms (otalgia <48 hours, temperature <39°C [102.2°F]), the American Academy of Pediatrics guideline supports either immediate antibiotic therapy or observation with close follow-up, depending on shared decision-making and reliable access to care. Given the moderate bulging, documented fever, and need for parental work-up the next day if symptoms persist, immediate treatment is reasonable, though observation remains guideline-concordant if the family prefers deferring antibiotics.

Immediate management

Analgesia (regardless of antibiotic decision):

Acetaminophen (Tylenol) 180–200 mg (15 mg/kg; maximum 75 mg/kg/day) every 4–6 hours as needed for pain/fever.
Ibuprofen (Advil, Motrin) 120–150 mg (10 mg/kg; maximum 40 mg/kg/day) every 6–8 hours if adequate hydration maintained; avoid if dehydrated or vomiting.

Antibiotic therapy (if chosen or if symptoms worsen within 48–72 hours on observation):

First-line: Amoxicillin 80–90 mg/kg/day divided twice daily (high-dose) for 7–10 days. At 12.4 kg: 550–600 mg orally twice daily for 10 days preferred given age borderline and moderate bulging.
Practical dosing: 600 mg (12 mL of 250 mg/5 mL suspension or 7.5 mL of 400 mg/5 mL suspension) twice daily.
Alternative if high risk for beta-lactamase organisms (daycare attendee, antibiotic within past 30 days, age <2 years): Amoxicillin-clavulanate (Augmentin) ES-600 formulation 90 mg/kg/day of amoxicillin component divided twice daily (600 mg twice daily).

Observation option (watchful waiting):

Provide a "safety-net" prescription to fill only if not improved within 48–72 hours or if worsening.
Return precautions: worsening pain, fever >39°C, decreased activity/lethargy, ear drainage, or failure to improve by 72 hours.
Diagnostic clarification and follow-up

No additional testing is required for uncomplicated AOM. If otorrhea develops, obtain culture if draining material is accessible. Re-evaluate in 24–48 hours if observing
