Analgesia (do this regardless of the antibiotic decision)

Pain and fever control is the cornerstone of AOM care and should be offered to all patients. Weight-based acetaminophen (10–15 mg/kg every 4–6 h; ~125–185 mg/dose at 12.4 kg) and/or ibuprofen (10 mg/kg every 6–8 h; ~125 mg/dose) are appropriate. Counsel the mother that fever and ear-tugging may take 2–3 days to fully settle even with treatment.

Antibiotic decision — two evidence-based options

This case sits in the gray zone: he is ≥24 months with mild-to-moderate, unilateral AOM, so guidelines permit observation with a rescue prescription if reliable 48–72 h follow-up and shared decision-making are assured. Most nonsevere AOM resolves spontaneously, and antibiotics cause diarrhea/rash in a meaningful minority (NNH ~9–15).
