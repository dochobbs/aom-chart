Immediate management
No laboratory studies or imaging are indicated for uncomplicated AOM diagnosed clinically by symptoms and otoscopy.

At 24 months, unilateral AOM with mild otalgia for less than 48 hours and temperature below 39°C qualifies for either immediate antibiotics or observation with close follow-up.
