Immediate management

Analgesia — Offer acetaminophen (Tylenol) 10–15 mg/kg orally every 4–6 hours as needed (maximum 100 mg/kg/day) or ibuprofen 10 mg/kg orally every 6–8 hours as needed for children >6 months. For a 12.4 kg child, acetaminophen 160 mg/5 mL suspension: 4–6 mL per dose; ibuprofen 100 mg/5 mL suspension: 6.25 mL per dose

Antibiotics — Given age 24 months, unilateral disease with moderate symptoms (fever ~38.7°C, bulging TM), initiate high-dose amoxicillin (Amoxil):

Dose: 90 mg/kg/day divided twice daily = approximately 560 mg per dose
Formulation: 400 mg/5 mL suspension
Volume: 7 mL orally twice daily for 10 days
Rationale: High-dose amoxicillin (80–90 mg/kg/day) achieves middle ear fluid concentrations that overcome penicillin-resistant Streptococcus pneumoniae
