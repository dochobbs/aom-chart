Antibiotic decision

Antibiotics are not routinely required for every preschool-aged child with pneumonia-like symptoms, because viruses are the most common cause in children 2–5 years of age.

Disposition and follow-up

If repeat assessment shows stable oxygenation, no sustained respiratory distress, preserved perfusion, adequate oral intake, and reliable follow-up, outpatient treatment is reasonable.
