This is a presumed febrile urinary tract infection (UTI), with possible upper-tract involvement, in a well-appearing child who is appropriate for outpatient oral therapy.

CLINICAL GUIDELINE

Begin antimicrobial treatment after obtaining the catheterized urine culture; confirm the diagnosis with pyuria plus growth of at least 50,000 colony-forming units/mL of a single uropathogen. Treat for 7–14 days, obtain renal–bladder ultrasonography after the first febrile UTI, and reserve voiding cystourethrography for an abnormal ultrasound, atypical/complex infection, or recurrent febrile UTI.
