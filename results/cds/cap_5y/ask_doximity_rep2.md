Evaluating outpatient management in pediatric pneumonia with hypoxemia

The 2023 IDSA guidelines recommend close outpatient monitoring for children with SpO2 93% and no signs of respiratory failure, but emphasize that supplemental oxygen and clinical reassessment within 24 hours are essential. The decision hinges on home support, access to follow-up, and the presence of comorbidities.

Red flags requiring urgent reassessment

Return immediately or seek ED evaluation for:

SpO2 <92% on repeat check, or any cyanosis
Increased work of breathing (retractions, nasal flaring, grunting)
Altered mental status, lethargy, or inability to arouse
Inability to tolerate oral intake or signs of dehydration
Persistent or worsening fever beyond 72 hours of antibiotics
