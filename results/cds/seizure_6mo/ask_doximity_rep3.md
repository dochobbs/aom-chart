Assessment

Most likely diagnosis: Simple febrile seizure. The infant meets all criteria: age 6–60 months, fever ≥100.4°F (38.9°C), generalized tonic-clonic activity, duration <15 minutes, single episode, and return to baseline within 1 hour.

Seizure first aid:

Do not restrain the child or place objects in the mouth.
Place child on side on floor/flat surface, clear nearby objects.
Time the seizure.
