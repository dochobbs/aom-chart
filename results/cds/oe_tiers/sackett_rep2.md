Pain control first (applies to both pathways)

Ensure adequate, scheduled analgesia — this is the intervention that most reliably improves how he feels. Rationale: most AOM in vaccinated children resolves spontaneously; antibiotics give only a modest symptom benefit (NNT ~20 for pain at 2–3 days, ~7 by 10–12 days) and cause an adverse event—usually diarrhea or rash—in roughly 1 of every 14 treated.
