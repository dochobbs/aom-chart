Evaluating antibiotic stewardship in unilateral AOM

The 2023 AAP guidelines support either immediate antibiotics or observation with close follow-up for children 24 months and older with unilateral acute otitis media and non-severe symptoms, with high-dose amoxicillin 80–90 mg/kg/day as first-line therapy if treatment is chosen. The validated symptom severity score requires documentation of fever threshold, pain duration, and activity level to classify symptom severity. Shared decision-making is essential, with treatment selection guided by parental preference and clinical context.

Immediate actions

Pain control is the priority regardless of antibiotic strategy. Recommend scheduled analgesia:

Acetaminophen (Tylenol) 15 mg/kg = 186 mg (approximately 5.5–6 mL of 160 mg/5 mL liquid) every 4–6 hours as needed.
Ibuprofen 10 mg/kg = 124 mg (approximately 6 mL of 100 mg/5 mL liquid) every 6–8 hours as needed; acceptable given age >6 months and adequate hydration.
Antibiotic therapy vs. observation

If the family prefers immediate antibiotics or if reliable follow-up is uncertain, prescribe high-dose amoxicillin (Amoxil) 80–90 mg/kg/day divided twice daily
