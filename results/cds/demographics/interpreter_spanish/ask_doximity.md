Counseling and safety netting

With the interpreter present, address:

Natural history: Most AOM in this age group resolves spontaneously within 48–72 hours; antibiotics modestly reduce duration of symptoms (about 1 day) but increase diarrhea and rash.
Observation safety: If choosing observation, provide a safety-net antibiotic prescription or ensure return within 48–72 hours if fever persists or pain worsens.
Return precautions: Seek care for persistent fever >48 hours, worsening ear pain, new ear discharge, lethargy, poor oral intake, or severe irritability.
Language: Ensure all instructions and return precautions are provided in Spanish with interpreter verification of understanding.
