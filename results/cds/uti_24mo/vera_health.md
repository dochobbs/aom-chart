This is a presumed first febrile urinary tract infection (UTI), likely upper-tract infection, despite the absence of flank tenderness; toddlers often cannot localize urinary symptoms. The positive nitrite, leukocyte esterase, pyuria, and bacteriuria from a catheterized specimen justify empiric treatment while awaiting culture.
