Assessing high-dose amoxicillin dosing for unilateral AOM in a 24-month-old

The AAP guidelines recommend high-dose amoxicillin at 80–90 mg/kg/day in two divided doses for acute otitis media in children aged 24 months, especially with fever or bulging tympanic membrane. For a 12.4 kg child, this translates to approximately 558 mg per dose, which can be administered as 6.25 mL of 400 mg/5 mL suspension twice daily.
