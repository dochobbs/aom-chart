Antibiotic choice and dosing

High-dose amoxicillin, 80–90 mg/kg/day divided BID, is first-line. No recent antibiotics, no purulent conjunctivitis, and no penicillin allergy are noted, so amoxicillin (not amoxicillin-clavulanate) is appropriate.

Pain and fever control (emphasize to all AOM patients)

Scheduled analgesia is central to management, not an afterthought. Weight-based acetaminophen (15 mg/kg q4–6h; ~185 mg per dose) and/or ibuprofen (10 mg/kg q6–8h; ~124 mg per dose, if ≥6 months and hydrating well) around the clock for the first 48 hours rather than PRN only.
