CLINICAL GUIDELINE

For a first febrile UTI in a child aged 2–24 months, obtain an appropriately collected urine culture, treat for 7–14 days, arrange renal–bladder ultrasonography, and do not perform routine voiding cystourethrography (VCUG) unless the ultrasound is abnormal, the infection is atypical/complex, or a recurrent febrile UTI occurs.
