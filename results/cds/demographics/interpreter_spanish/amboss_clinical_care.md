Pain and fever management
Give acetaminophen or ibuprofen for otalgia and fever, using the product’s weight-based pediatric instructions. These medications are considered mainstays of pain management for AOM. 
2
Continue analgesia whether or not antibiotics are prescribed because antibiotics generally do not relieve pain during the first 24 hours. 
2
Do not use opioids, decongestants, antihistamines, or steroids routinely for uncomplicated AOM. 
1
Avoid topical ear drops unless the TM is confirmed intact and the product is specifically appropriate; no topical analgesic is needed for this presentation.
