Evaluating antibiotic decision in pediatric AOM

AAP guidelines for acute otitis media in children aged 24 months require assessing symptom severity using fever ≥39°C or pain lasting ≥48 hours to determine antibiotic use. The validated score for symptom severity in AOM includes temperature threshold and duration of otalgia, with inputs of fever measurement and pain duration.

Assessing amoxicillin dosing in pediatric AOM

The recommended dose for amoxicillin in acute otitis media is 80 to 90 mg per kg per day in two divided doses, with a maximum of 500 mg per dose. For pain control, acetaminophen or ibuprofen dosing should be based on weight with a standard pediatric formulation.
