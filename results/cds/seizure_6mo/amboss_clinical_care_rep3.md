Treatment of the febrile illness

Provide fluids according to intake and hydration status, light clothing, and an antipyretic if she is uncomfortable. Antipyretics improve comfort but do not reliably prevent future febrile seizures.

I would observe her through a period of continued clinical stability and arrange emergency department evaluation, with pediatric or neurologic consultation if the event is judged prolonged, atypical, recurrent, or not clearly generalized.
