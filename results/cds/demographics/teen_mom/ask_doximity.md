AAP 2013 guidelines define severe symptoms in acute otitis media as fever ≥39°C, severe ear pain, or toxic appearance; this child has 38.7°C fever and mild discomfort, meeting criteria for observation in the ≥24 months age group. Evaluating pain control in acute otitis media management

Effective analgesia is a cornerstone of care regardless of antibiotic choice, with acetaminophen or ibuprofen providing reliable symptom relief in children with AOM. Pain reduction improves comfort, supports hydration, and may reduce parental anxiety during the course of illness.
