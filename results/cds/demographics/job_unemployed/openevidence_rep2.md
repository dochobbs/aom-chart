Pain control is the top priority regardless of the antibiotic decision — scheduled/PRN acetaminophen or ibuprofen is recommended for all children with AOM.

 Ibuprofen (10 mg/kg, ~120 mg per dose) can be added or alternated for better coverage than the single overnight acetaminophen dose he received.

Antibiotic decision:

If treating now (reasonable given fever + distinct bulging): high-dose amoxicillin 80–90 mg/kg/day divided BID is first-line since he has had no amoxicillin in the prior 30 days, no purulent conjunctivitis, and no penicillin allergy. At 12.4 kg that is roughly 500 mg PO BID (~1000 mg/day ≈ 81 mg/kg/day).
