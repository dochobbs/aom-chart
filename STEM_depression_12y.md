# Locked stem — adolescent depression, 12 years

`packet_version`: 1

Diagnosis is made in the chart. The only fork is management.
Unlike AOM, Mode 3 is live here: starting an SSRI with item 9 unknown is a harmful commission, not a legal alternative.
Age, sex, historian, symptom set, duration, PHQ-A total, exam, assessment, and the plan ask are frozen.
Sentence 1 is the only HPI line that variants may rewrite (`opener`).
