Evaluating febrile seizure classification in infancy

AAP guidelines define simple febrile seizures as generalized, lasting less than 15 minutes, with full recovery within 1 hour and no focal features. This case involves a 6-month-old with a bilateral tonic-clonic event lasting approximately 9 minutes from partial observation, now alert and consolable, but duration uncertainty remains. Validated criteria for febrile seizure classification require documented duration from onset, post-ictal recovery, and seizure morphology—inputs needed for clinical decision rules.
